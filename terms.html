What STANZA is

STANZA (0xf629f16ba91086e266accc417d10742f76207ba3) is an ERC-20 token on the Base blockchain. Its purpose is recognition: honoring scholars for manuscripts and datasets, and letting readers license that work directly. Amounts are counted in STANZA, never in dollars.

An earlier deployment, $SPIN (0x4c972afefa2e4552307b87cf944e53136c361ba3), launched September 27, 2026, never saw a single trade, and is superseded. For anything you do, verify, or hold, STANZA is the token.

What is live and what is not

Live: the STANZA token contract, the Uniswap V4 pool position, and this trust layer. Not yet live: the repository itself — scholar uploads, review, and reader licensing arrive in Phases 1 and 2. The mechanism pages describe the frozen spec: what is decided, not what is shipped.

The pool has seen no trades since deployment. Until a market forms, there is no price.
