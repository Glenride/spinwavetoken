How recognition works

SpinWave is becoming a scholarly repository: scholars upload manuscripts and datasets, and the clever recognize their own — in SPIN. Readers will pay SPIN to browse and license data. That is the whole economy, and it is deliberately simple.

The repository opens in Phase 1. Until then, any recognition recorded for scholars is pending — counted, not paid. Reader licensing follows in Phase 2. Amounts are always counted in SPIN, never in dollars. We do not promise what a token is worth; we only promise to keep the books straight.

What “pending” means

Pending means reserved, not paid. Nothing has been sent to you and nothing is held in an account in your name. It is not a balance and is never shown as one.

The form and timing of recognition are set by the terms published when Phase 1 opens — not by anything written before. If Phase 1 never opens, pending recognition has no value.

    Trail-marks

    Your trail-mark is derived from your wallet — share it, and the den knows who walked you in. Trail-marks carry recognition, not payment: they mark standing among the clever, never a balance. One walker, one mark.

    Privacy

    Connecting a wallet shares two things: your address and your on-chain SPIN balance — that is all we read, and we read it only to show your standing. We never see, ask for, or store anything else. No email is collected on this site.

    Security

    SpinWave will never ask for a seed phrase or a private key. Scholar recognition and anything claimable in the future happen only on this site, signed in with your own wallet. Messages offering early recognition, bonus SPIN, or “support” are not from us.

    Risks

    Said plainly, because the fox respects a reader who reads:

    • No market yet. The pool has seen zero trades. Price discovery has not happened, and there is no price to point at.
    • A token can lose some or all of its value. Anything counted in SPIN carries that risk.
    • Code can fail. The token, the pool, and the vesting reserve are smart contracts, and smart contracts can have bugs.
    • The vesting reserve's release mechanics are not yet published. 15B SPIN sits in the token contract; how it unlocks is undecided.
    • The pool is paired with TAO. TAO's own price moves will move the pair.
    • The repository does not exist yet. Scholar recognition and reader licensing are Phase 1 and Phase 2. Pending is not a balance.
    • Nothing here is financial advice, and holding SPIN is not an investment.
