## How recognition works
SpinWave is becoming a **scholarly repository**: scholars upload manuscripts and datasets, and the clever recognize their own — in STANZA. Readers will pay SPIN to browse and license data. That is the whole economy, and it is deliberately simple.
The repository opens in **Phase 1**. Until then, any recognition recorded for scholars is **pending** — counted, not paid. Reader licensing follows in **Phase 2**. Amounts are always counted in STANZA, never in dollars. We do not promise what a token is worth; we only promise to keep the books straight.

## What “pending” means
**Pending means reserved, not paid.** Nothing has been sent to you and nothing is held in an account in your name. It is not a balance and is never shown as one.
The form and timing of recognition are set by the terms published when Phase 1 opens — not by anything written before. **If Phase 1 never opens, pending recognition has no value.**

## Trail-marks
Your trail-mark is derived from your wallet — share it, and the den knows who walked you in. Trail-marks carry **recognition, not payment**: they mark standing among the clever, never a balance. One walker, one mark.
## Privacy
Connecting a wallet shares two things: **your address and your on-chain STANZA balance** — that is all we read, and we read it only to show your standing. We never see, ask for, or store anything else. No email is collected on this site.
## Security
**SpinWave will never ask for a seed phrase or a private key.** Scholar recognition and anything claimable in the future happen only on this site, signed in with your own wallet. Messages offering early recognition, bonus STANZA, or “support” are not from us.
## Risks

- **The repository does not exist yet.** Scholar recognition and reader licensing are Phase 1 and Phase 2. Pending is not a balance.
