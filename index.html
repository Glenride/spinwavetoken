The Tale of Reynard

The straight line.

SpinWave is the database and storage layer of the Glenride think tank. STANZA is its mode of data transfer, represented by monetary value or by attested certificates, for recognizing scholars. Scholars earn it for manuscripts and datasets freed from paywalls; readers will use it to license that work directly.

Recognition

  • 40,000 STANZA — accepted dataset
  • 55,000 STANZA — dataset with a worked example
  • 60% of every reader license — straight to the scholar
