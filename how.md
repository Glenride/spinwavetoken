## HOW IT WILL WORK
The mechanism, step by step — scholars in, readers in, money moving. Decided, not built. When it changes, the trust center changes with it.
## What the chain holds
The blockchain is the cash register and the notary — not the library.
The money
STANZA — payments settle on Base.
The proof
A Glenride attestation: fingerprint, timestamp, and verifier. Proof the work is what it claims to be.
The license
Who paid whom, for what rights.
The manuscript or dataset itself lives in normal off-chain storage, held by Glenride.
## The two flows
Phase 1 · Scholars
### Publish and get paid
- 1**Connect a wallet** on the repository.
- 2**Upload** the manuscript or dataset.
- 3**Get attested.** Glenride verifies authorship and claims. The attestation travels with the work — readers see the proof before they pay.
- 4**Set license tiers** — read, commercial, exclusive.
- 5**Share the page.** The scholar markets their own link.
- 6**Get paid in STANZA** on every license — notified each time.
Phase 2 · Readers
### One page, one price, one button
- 1**Browse** the repository.
- 2**See the terms** — what it is, what it costs, what the license allows.
- 3**Pay STANZA.**
- 4**Access** per the license — read, or reuse commercially.
## License tiers
Read
Pay STANZA to read and browse the work.
Commercial
Pay STANZA to reuse the work commercially.
Exclusive
Negotiated — including embargo-style timed exclusives: one outlet, one window, terms visible to everyone. A priced product, not a backroom deal.
**Operated by Glenride.** Glenride holds the manuscripts and runs the platform day to day. Changes to this mechanism go through consensus of the larger Glenride body — and the trust center stays the public record of what is live versus planned.
This spec was first drafted for $SPIN; the economy now runs on **STANZA** — the token going forward. The $SPIN deployment is superseded, kept on record at the [token page](https://spinwave.tech/token.html).
